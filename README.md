
# PitchLab
### A Data-Driven Pitcher Development Optimization System

## Project Overview

PitchLab is a baseball analytics research project designed to help identify potential improvements for struggling pitchers through data-driven analysis.

The goal is to develop a system that analyzes relationships between pitching mechanics, pitch characteristics, and performance to identify realistic adjustments that may improve a pitcher's effectiveness.

Rather than assuming there is one ideal pitching profile, PitchLab aims to recognize multiple successful approaches and determine which development paths are most attainable for individual pitchers.

## Research Objective

**Can historical pitching data be used to identify realistic changes to a pitcher's mechanics and pitch characteristics that are associated with improved performance?**

## Model Architecture

### Model 1: Pitching Relationships
Analyze relationships between pitching mechanics and pitch characteristics, including arm angle, release point, extension, velocity, spin, and movement.

### Model 2: Successful Pitching Profiles
Identify multiple combinations of pitching characteristics associated with successful outcomes, recognizing that different pitchers can achieve success through different approaches.

### Model 3: Individualized Development Pathways
Identify which successful profiles are most attainable for a particular pitcher and evaluate potential adjustments to move toward those profiles.

## Future Development

Planned functionality includes pitcher diagnostics, predictive adjustment modeling, historical validation, and an interactive visualization system.

## Limitations

PitchLab is intended as a decision-support and research tool. Historical associations do not establish that a mechanical adjustment will cause improvement. Recommendations will require validation and consideration of player-specific physical and coaching constraints.
